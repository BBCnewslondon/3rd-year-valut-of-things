$$V(r)=-G\int \frac{\rho(r')}{|r-r'|}d^3 r'$$
If we then then taylor expand the $\frac{1}{|r-r'|}$ term we would get an infinite series given by

$$\frac{1}{|r-r'|}=\frac{1}{r}\sum^\infty_{n=0} \left( \frac{r'}{r} \right)P_{n}\cos(\alpha)$$

$$V_{quad}(r)\propto \frac{1}{r^3}\sum_{i,j}\hat{r}_{i}\hat{r}_{j}Q_{i,j}$$
